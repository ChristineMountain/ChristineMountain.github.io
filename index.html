🏆 Best Insights, ASA DataFest 2026

ASA DataFest ASU/University of Arizona

Python SQL DuckDB Excel Machine Learning Visualization

Under a 48-hour deadline, our team (Machine Learning Learning Machines) analyzed a multi-million-row healthcare dataset to find where access to care breaks down. We modeled how patient visits fall off with distance from the hospital (about a 2% drop per mile within ~21 miles) to help pinpoint where new clinics would have the biggest impact, and found that patients with chronic conditions who used the MyChart portal averaged roughly 12 more visits over four years. Presenting those findings to industry professionals and statisticians earned us Best Insights among competing teams.

🎤 SUnMaRC Presenter, Spring 2026

Battleship Neuroevolution

Python Neural Networks Neuroevolution Simulation

I built a neural network that learns where to shoot next on a 10×10 Battleship board, trained through neuroevolution instead of traditional backpropagation: each generation of candidate networks played many simulated games, and the strongest performers passed their weights (with small mutations) on to the next generation. Each board cell was scored using 20 engineered features, including hit/miss history, remaining ship-length probabilities, and board position. Across 30 generations of training, the resulting network averaged 45.5 moves per win, far better than random play (97) or hit-chasing (67), and close to the theoretical probabilistic-search optimum (42), while running orders of magnitude faster. I presented this research at SUnMaRC as the University of Arizona's only female student presenter.
